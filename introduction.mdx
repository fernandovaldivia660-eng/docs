El Piso es Lava is a browser-based party game referee that turns any room into a lava-filled arena. Open a single HTML file, add your players, and let the Spanish voice announcer call out safe phases, warnings, and lava strikes — no app store, no account, and no installation needed. Whether you are playing with two friends or a full group of eight, the app handles the timing, scoring, and drama so you can focus on staying off the floor.

## What makes it unique

El Piso es Lava combines three things that most party timers lack: a live Spanish text-to-speech announcer that builds tension as lava approaches, an optional camera that watches the floor and automatically flags players whose color appears during a lava phase, and a collection of seven distinct objectives that keep every session feeling different. Because everything runs inside a single HTML file, the game works completely offline after your first load — no Wi-Fi required mid-game.

## System requirements

- **Browser**: Safari or Chrome on any modern device (desktop, tablet, or phone)
- **Voice**: a Spanish TTS voice must be installed on your device for the announcer to speak; most iOS and Android devices include one by default
- **Camera** *(optional)*: HTTPS connection or a local file URL is required for camera access; a standard front or rear camera is sufficient
- **Internet**: only needed on first load; the game runs fully offline after that

<Note>
  On iPhone, the **mute switch on the side of your device must be OFF** (not showing orange). Even at full volume, iOS silences the voice announcer when the mute switch is engaged.
</Note>
