Every round of El Piso es Lava follows the same lifecycle, but no two rounds feel identical. The phase cycle keeps players guessing, Traición mode throws in fake-outs and sneak attacks, and the in-game controls let you manage the action without breaking the flow. Here's everything that happens from the moment you tap **Empezar** to the moment the final score appears.

## Full round lifecycle

<Steps>
  <Step title="Setup screen">
    Configure the round: choose your objective, set the pace, toggle **3 vidas por jugador**, enable or disable Traición mode, and add or remove players. When everything looks right, tap **Empezar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Countdown">
    The voice announcer counts down **3 — 2 — 1 — ¡YA!** while the screen counts down visually. Use these seconds to get into position — the first phase starts the instant the countdown ends.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Voice announces the objective">
    Right after ¡YA!, the announcer calls out the objective so every player knows exactly what they're playing for this round.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Phase cycle begins">
    Safe and lava phases alternate repeatedly until the timer expires or a win condition is met. See the phase cycle diagram below for the full breakdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ten-second warning">
    When **10 seconds** remain on the clock, the voice says **¡Diez segundos!** — a cue to push hard for a last-second win or protect your lives.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Round ends">
    Time expires (or a cup/survival win condition is triggered) and the referee calls **endRound**. The result screen appears automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Result screen">
    See the round winner, the full scoreboard, and two buttons: **Siguiente ronda** to play again or **Terminar partida** to end the session and see final standings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Phase cycle

The referee cycles through phases continuously during play. Each phase has a distinct colour and audio cue so you always know where you stand.

```
🟢 Safe phase
      │
      ▼
🟡 Warning phase  ──(Traición: Falsa alarma)──▶  🟢 Safe phase
      │
      ▼
🔴 Lava phase
      │
      ▼
🟢 Safe phase
      │
     (repeat)
```

- **Safe phase (green):** the floor is safe. Move freely, grab items, reposition.
- **Warning phase (amber):** lava is coming — get off the floor now. This phase is optional depending on pace settings; faster paces may shorten or skip it.
- **Lava phase (red):** the floor is lava. Any floor contact costs a life (or eliminates you instantly in strict mode).

<Note>
  The length of each phase varies with the **pace** setting you chose at setup. A faster pace means shorter safe windows and quicker lava cycles.
</Note>

## Traición mode variations

When **Traición** mode is enabled, the referee can break the normal pattern in two ways.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Falsa alarma (fake-out)" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    A warning phase builds tension… then resolves to **safe** instead of lava. The voice calls it out as a false alarm. If you dived onto the sofa for nothing, that's on the game — not you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sneak lava" icon="fire">
    The safe phase jumps **directly to lava** with no warning at all. No amber, no countdown — just sudden red. Stay alert even when everything looks calm.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  In Traición mode you can never fully relax during a safe phase. A sneak lava can fire at any moment. Keep one foot near higher ground.
</Warning>

## In-game controls

During play, a controls bar stays visible at the top of the screen. These are your live tools for managing the round.

| Icon | Action |
|---|---|
| **⏸** | Pause / resume the round. The phase cycle freezes; no lives can be lost while paused. |
| **🗣️** | Toggle the voice announcer on or off. SFX continue independently. |
| **🔊** | Toggle sound effects on or off. Voice continues independently. |
| **↩** | Undo the last life deduction. Tap once per deduction you want to reverse. |

<Tip>
  Tap ↩ immediately after a contested call — once the phase changes and more action happens, it becomes harder to track exactly which deduction you want to reverse.
</Tip>

## PiP camera overlay

When camera mode is active, a **Picture-in-Picture (PiP)** overlay appears in the top-right corner of the screen. It shows the live camera feed with coloured dots marking each tracked player.

- Dots change colour to reflect each player's assigned colour.
- A dot on a floor cell during lava is what triggers the auto-deduction.
- Tap the overlay to expand the camera view if you need a closer look at a disputed call.

<Info>
  If a player's dot isn't appearing correctly, check that their colour assignment on the setup screen matches what they're actually wearing or holding. Lighting changes can affect detection.
</Info>

## Result screen breakdown

After a round ends, the result screen gives you a full summary:

- **Kicker text** — a short flavour line from the announcer (e.g. "¡El suelo era lava!").
- **Winner name** — the round victor highlighted at the top, or "Empate" for a draw.
- **Score summary** — the live scoreboard showing wins, items, and steps for every player.
- **Siguiente ronda** — resets lives and items, carries forward win counts, and returns to the setup screen.
- **Terminar partida** — closes the session, triggers the voice announcement of the overall champion, and shows final standings.

<Note>
  When you tap **Siguiente ronda**, win counts from all previous rounds remain intact. Only per-round values (lives, items collected that round) reset.
</Note>
