El Piso es Lava uses your browser's built-in Speech Synthesis API to narrate the game in Spanish. Choosing the right voice makes the experience more immersive, and installing a high-quality Spanish voice on your device ensures you hear every countdown and warning clearly.

## How the voice selector works

When you open the voice settings panel, you'll see a dropdown list of all voices available on your device. The list is organized into two groups:

- **Spanish voices** — shown at the top, sorted by quality preference.
- **Otros idiomas (acento raro)** — all remaining voices, listed below a divider. These can still speak Spanish, but they may sound accented or robotic.

Within the Spanish voices group, the app automatically prefers voices in this order:

1. A voice with a female name
2. A Google-branded voice (e.g., *Google español*)
3. A voice with an `es-MX`, `es-ES`, or `es-US` locale
4. Any other voice tagged as Spanish
5. Your device's system default (fallback)

<Tip>
  Google TTS voices (labeled *Google español* or similar) tend to sound noticeably more natural and expressive than the built-in system voices. If you see one in the list, it's worth selecting it first.
</Tip>

## Picking the best voice

Open the voice selector before your first game and work through this checklist:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open voice settings">
    Tap the settings icon and locate the **Voz** (voice) dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Spanish voice from the top group">
    Select any voice in the Spanish section. Female voices and Google voices typically sound the most natural for game narration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the voice">
    Tap **Probar voz y sonido**. The app plays a short test phrase followed by a sound effect so you can judge both the voice tone and the device volume at the same time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust if needed">
    If the voice sounds wrong — too robotic, wrong language, or silent — try a different entry in the list or install a Spanish voice following the steps below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Installing a Spanish voice

If the Spanish voices group is empty or only shows a fallback device default, you need to install a Spanish voice at the operating-system level. The app cannot install voices for you, but the process is straightforward on both major platforms.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="iPhone / iPad" icon="apple">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Settings">
        Go to **Settings** on your device.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Accessibility → Spoken Content">
        Tap **Accessibility**, then **Spoken Content**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open Voices">
        Tap **Voices** to see the language list.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Select Spanish">
        Tap **Spanish** (Español) and choose a regional variant, such as *Spanish (Mexico)* or *Spanish (Spain)*.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Download a voice">
        Tap any listed voice and then tap the download icon ⬇️ next to it. Enhanced voices take a moment to download over Wi-Fi.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Reload the app">
        Return to El Piso es Lava and refresh the page. The new voice will appear in the selector.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Card>
  <Card title="Android" icon="android">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open Settings">
        Go to **Settings** on your device.
      </Step>
      <Step title="General Management → Language">
        Tap **General Management** (on Samsung) or **System**, then **Language & input**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Text-to-speech output">
        Tap **Text-to-speech output** (or **Text-to-speech**).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open engine settings">
        Tap the gear icon ⚙️ next to your preferred TTS engine (usually Google TTS).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Install Spanish language data">
        Tap **Install voice data**, choose **Spanish**, and download the language pack.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Reload the app">
        Return to El Piso es Lava and refresh the page. Spanish voices will now appear at the top of the selector.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Toggling voice during play

Once the game is running, you can turn narration on or off at any time without interrupting the round.

- Tap the **🗣️** icon in the play screen to toggle the voice on or off.
- When you pause the game, any speech currently in progress is cancelled immediately so the voice doesn't keep talking over the pause screen.
- Your toggle preference is saved automatically, so it persists the next time you open the app.

<Note>
  On iPhone, the physical **mute / silent switch** on the side of the device must be in the **OFF** (unmuted) position. Even if your system volume is turned up, iOS silences Speech Synthesis when the silent switch is active, so you'll hear nothing during play.
</Note>

## Voice rate and pitch

The app intentionally speaks slightly faster and at a higher pitch than the system default — this adds energy and urgency to the narration that suits the pace of the game. You do not need to adjust these values manually; they are set automatically.

- **Rate:** between 1.02× and 1.15× normal speed — just brisk enough to feel snappy without becoming hard to understand.
- **Pitch:** between 1.3 and 1.45 — noticeably higher than a neutral voice, which reinforces the excited, playful tone of the game.

## Screen and voice during play

While a round is in progress, the app requests a **wake lock** so your screen stays on automatically — you won't need to keep tapping the screen to prevent it from going dark.

On mobile browsers, the Speech Synthesis API can fall silent after a period of inactivity. To prevent this, the app sends a keep-alive signal every 7 seconds while the game is running, so narration continues reliably throughout long rounds even on devices that normally suspend audio in the background.
