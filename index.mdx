## How it works

El Piso es Lava runs entirely in your browser — no download, no account, no internet required after the page loads. Open the file, configure your game, hit **Empezar**, and the voice referee takes over.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the game">
    Open `piso-es-lava.html` in Safari or Chrome on your phone. For best results, serve it over a local HTTPS connection so the camera and voice work without restrictions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add players">
    Enter each player's name (up to 8 players). Each player is automatically assigned a unique color — they'll wear or hold something that color during the game.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your mode">
    Choose an objective (grab the cup, survive, scavenger hunt…), a difficulty pace, and a round duration. Toggle camera tracking on if you want automatic lava detection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hit Empezar">
    The countdown runs, the voice announces the objective, and the game begins. The referee calls safe phases, warnings, and lava events automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>
