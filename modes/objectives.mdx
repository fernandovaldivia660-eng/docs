Every round of El Piso es Lava is built around a single objective that decides what you do during the safe phases and what winning looks like. Choosing the right objective is just as important as choosing the right difficulty — it sets the whole vibe of the party.

## 1. La Copa — Cup Race

**Spanish name:** La Copa
**English:** The Cup

A single cup is placed somewhere in the room before the round begins. When the safe phase starts, everyone races to grab it. The first player to pick up the cup wins the round.

**How you win:** Be the first player to physically grab the cup during a safe phase.

<Note>
  If you have camera detection enabled, the app can automatically recognise when the cup is lifted and award the round instantly — no arguments, no disputes.
</Note>

**Key rules:**
- The cup must stay in the designated spot at the start of each round — no pre-positioning.
- Grabbing the cup during a lava phase disqualifies that attempt; put it back.
- If two players grab simultaneously, the camera (or a human referee) decides.

## 2. Ida y Vuelta — There and Back

**Spanish name:** Ida y Vuelta
**English:** Round Trip

This objective builds on La Copa with a second requirement: after you grab the cup, you must carry it back to your own base zone without dropping it. Only reaching your base with the cup in hand wins the round.

**How you win:** Pick up the cup AND return it to your base — without dropping it — before lava hits again.

<Warning>
  If you drop the cup at any point during your return, it must be placed back at the original cup spot before the next attempt. Watch your footing on furniture.
</Warning>

**Key rules:**
- Each player's base zone must be agreed upon before the game starts (e.g., a specific cushion or chair).
- Camera tracking can detect if the cup leaves your hands mid-run.
- If lava hits while you are carrying the cup, you lose a life and must return the cup to its starting position.

## 3. Cacería — Item Hunt

**Spanish name:** Cacería
**English:** Hunt

The voice calls out a random item — for example, *'un zapato'* (a shoe), *'algo azul'* (something blue), or *'una cuchara'* (a spoon). Everyone scrambles to be the first to bring that item back. The player who collects the most items by the time the round ends wins.

**How you win:** Collect more fetched items than any other player when the round timer expires.

<Info>
  A new item is called out every 26 seconds. If nobody manages to fetch the current item in time, the voice announces a new one — so no single item blocks the whole round.
</Info>

**Key rules:**
- You must physically present the item (or drop it in a designated collection zone) to score it.
- Items collected during a lava phase do not count — you must be elevated when lava is active.
- Ties are broken by who collected their last item first.

## 4. Pasa la Copa — Hot Potato

**Spanish name:** Pasa la Copa
**English:** Pass the Cup / Hot Potato

Everyone passes a single cup around constantly. The goal is simple: do not be holding the cup when the lava phase starts. Whoever holds it when *'¡El piso es lava!'* is called loses a life.

**How you win:** Avoid holding the cup during lava phases and outlast all other players.

<Warning>
  You cannot put the cup down — it must always be in someone's hands. If you deliberately leave it on a surface between players, the nearest player is deemed the holder.
</Warning>

**Key rules:**
- The holder is tracked manually by default; enable the camera for automatic detection.
- You must still get off the floor during lava phases — holding the cup does not exempt you from the elevation rule.
- Losing a life from holding the cup and losing a life from being on the floor are two separate penalties that can happen in the same lava phase.

## 5. Estatuas — Statues

**Spanish name:** Estatuas
**English:** Statues

When lava hits, everyone must do two things: get off the floor *and* freeze completely. Any movement — even a stumble to keep your balance — costs you a life. The challenge is finding a stable elevated position during the safe phase and committing to it.

**How you win:** Keep more lives than anyone else by staying perfectly still on your elevated surface throughout every lava phase.

<Tip>
  Scout your frozen pose during the safe phase. Wide, low stances on stable furniture are much easier to hold than standing on a single cushion — especially during long lava phases on Volcán or Caos difficulty.
</Tip>

**Key rules:**
- Movement is judged visually — with camera detection, the app flags motion during lava phases automatically.
- Minor involuntary movements (a blink, breathing) do not count. Shifting your feet, reaching out an arm, or dropping something does.
- If you fall off your surface during a lava phase, you lose a life for both moving and being on the floor.

## 6. Cadena — Chain

**Spanish name:** Cadena
**English:** Chain

When lava hits, every player must be physically touching at least one other player — forming a connected chain. Anyone who is isolated (not touching another person) loses a life, even if they are correctly elevated.

**How you win:** Stay connected to the group during every lava phase while also staying elevated.

<Note>
  You only need to be touching one other person who is themselves part of the chain. A fingertip to a shoulder counts — but be careful not to pull each other off your surfaces.
</Note>

**Key rules:**
- The chain does not have to be a single line; any connected graph of players is valid.
- Contact must be maintained for the entire lava phase, not just the moment it starts.
- You still lose a life if you are touching the group but your feet are on the floor.

---

## 7. Supervivencia — Survival

**Spanish name:** Supervivencia
**English:** Survival

Pure, unadorned El Piso es Lava. There is no cup, no items, no chain, no freezing. Your only job is to get off the floor during every lava phase. The last player with lives remaining wins.

**How you win:** Be the final player who has not run out of lives.

<Info>
  Supervivencia is the best starting point for new players and younger kids. There is nothing to remember except one rule: when the floor is lava, get up.
</Info>

**Key rules:**
- Each player starts with the same number of lives (set in the game configuration).
- A player is eliminated when they reach zero lives.
- If multiple players run out of lives on the same phase, the player with the highest score up to that point is ranked higher.
