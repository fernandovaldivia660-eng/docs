The setup screen is where you shape the entire game before a single round begins. Every option you configure here — from player names to voice selection — applies to all rounds in the session. Take a minute to review each section below so nothing surprises you mid-game.

---

## Players

Add between **2 and 8 players** by typing a name into the player field and tapping **+**. As each player joins, the app automatically assigns them one of eight distinct colors:

| Color | Spanish name |
|-------|-------------|
| Pink | Rosa |
| Lime | Lima |
| Cyan | Cian |
| Orange | Naranja |
| Purple | Morado |
| Yellow | Amarillo |
| Green | Verde |
| Blue | Azul |

Each player should hold or wear a physical object in their assigned color — a colored cup, piece of tape, or clothing works well. The camera (if enabled) uses these colors to track players, and the announcer references them during the game.

<Tip>
  Colored plastic cups are a simple, cheap prop — one per player, matching their assigned color. They double as a drink holder for a true party setup.
</Tip>

---

## Objective

Tap the **Objetivo** selector to choose one of seven game modes. Each objective defines a different win condition and play style. See [Objectives](/modes/objectives) for a full description of every option:

- **Copa** — hold the cup when time runs out to win
- **Ida y vuelta** — pass the cup back and forth; most completions wins
- **Cacería** — hunters and prey; roles flip when the prey is tagged
- **Pasa la copa** — hot-potato passing under lava pressure
- **Estatuas** — freeze in place when lava hits; last player standing wins
- **Cadena** — players form a chain; break it and lose a life
- **Supervivencia** — classic last-player-standing elimination

## Difficulty (Ritmo de la lava)

The **Ritmo de la lava** selector controls how frequently lava phases arrive and how long they last. Eight difficulty levels are available, from leisurely warm-ups to punishing all-out chaos. See [Difficulty](/modes/difficulty) for a breakdown of each pace and recommendations for different group sizes.

<Tip>
  For a first game with new players, start on a lower difficulty so everyone has time to understand the phases before lava starts arriving fast.
</Tip>

---

## Round Duration (Duración)

Use the **Duración** control to set how long each round lasts. Shorter rounds keep energy high in large groups; longer rounds reward strategy and endurance. See [Round Length](/modes/round-length) for guidance on matching duration to your objective and group size.

---

## Voice

The **Voz** selector lets you pick which text-to-speech voice the announcer uses. Tap **Probar voz** after selecting a voice to hear a sample before the game starts.

<Info>
  The app requires a **Spanish-language TTS voice** to be installed on your device. Most iOS and Android devices include at least one Spanish voice by default. If the voice selector shows no options or the announcer is silent, go to your device's accessibility or language settings and download a Spanish voice.
</Info>

<Tip>
  On iOS, you can download higher-quality "Enhanced" Spanish voices under **Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Spanish**. They sound noticeably more natural during play.
</Tip>

## Rules toggles

### Aviso antes de la lava

When this toggle is **on** (default), the announcer gives a verbal warning before each lava phase — giving players a moment to scramble to safety. Turn it off for a harder, more unpredictable experience.

### 3 vidas por jugador

When this toggle is **on** (default), each player starts with three lives. Touching the floor during a lava phase costs one life; losing all three eliminates that player. Turn the toggle off to switch to **instant elimination** — one strike and you are out. See [Lives](/rules/lives) for more detail on how the lives system interacts with different objectives.

## Camera tracking

Toggle on **Seguimiento por cámara** to let the app monitor the floor through your device's camera and automatically detect when a player's color appears during a lava phase. After enabling the toggle, tap **Calibrar** to run the calibration process and teach the camera what the floor looks like.

<Tip>
  Place your device on a stable surface pointing at the main play area before calibrating. The more consistent the camera angle, the more reliable the color detection will be.
</Tip>

See [Camera Overview](/camera/overview) for a full explanation of how tracking works and [Camera Calibration](/camera/calibration) for step-by-step calibration instructions.

---

## Starting the game

When all options are set, tap **Empezar**. A countdown begins, the announcer speaks, and your first round is underway. You can always return to the setup screen between rounds by tapping **Terminar partida** from the score screen.
